# Evidence weighting

Prioritize direct source or execution evidence, reproducible observations, and method-contract violations over model confidence or eloquence. Distinguish factual disagreement, boundary disagreement, implementation disagreement, and preference disagreement.

A decision may remain deferred when the required experiment, authority, or source is unavailable. Deferral is preferable to invented certainty.

`defer` is still an explicit authoritative disposition for coverage accounting; it does not resolve the missing evidence. Preserve the required follow-up as a task or blocker rather than recasting deferral as acceptance.
