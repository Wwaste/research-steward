# Statistics audit checklist

- [ ] Primary estimand is named and matches the reported number.
- [ ] CI reported; width discussed if wide.
- [ ] Multiplicity family defined; correction or waiver justified.
- [ ] Missingness mechanism discussed; sensitivity to complete-case noted.
- [ ] No outcome leakage into features or early stopping without disclosure.
- [ ] Assumptions listed; tests or robust alternatives cited.
- [ ] Sensitivity / multiverse choices that could flip the claim are named.
