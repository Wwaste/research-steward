# Claim vs design boundaries

| Design | Allowed claim language | Not allowed without extra design |
|---|---|---|
| RCT | "caused" / "reduced" under protocol | long-term generalization beyond sample |
| Cohort / observational | "associated with" | "causes" without identification strategy |
| Case series / report | "observed in" | frequency in population |
| Simulation | "in the model" | empirical prevalence |
